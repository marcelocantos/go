When built with `GOEXPERIMENT=decimal`, the new [math/decimal] package provides
functions for the `decimal64` and `decimal128` types, including the IEEE 754
decimal operations that the operators do not provide, such as [Quantize],
[Reduce], [Quantum] and rounding with an explicit [RoundingMode]. It also
provides codecs between decimal values and the BID and DPD interchange
encodings, and reports the stored representation as [NativeRepresentation].

The package has the square root and the other operations of IEEE 754-2019
that arithmetic does not give: [Sqrt], [ScaleB], [LogB], [NextUp] and
[NextDown], and the correctly rounded [Exp], [Log], [Log10], [Pow], [Pown],
[Rootn] and [Compound].
