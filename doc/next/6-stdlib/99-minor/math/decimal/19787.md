When built with `GOEXPERIMENT=decimal`, the new [math/decimal] package provides
functions for the `decimal64` and `decimal128` types, including the IEEE 754
decimal operations that the operators do not provide, such as [Quantize],
[Reduce], [Quantum] and rounding with an explicit [RoundingMode]. It also
provides codecs between decimal values and the BID and DPD interchange
encodings, and [Native64bits], [Native64frombits], [Native128bits] and
[Native128frombits], which convert between decimal values and their exact
stored bits, whose meaning [NativeRepresentation] names.

The package has the square root and the other operations of IEEE 754-2019
that arithmetic does not give: [Sqrt], [Ldexp], [Logb], [Ilogb] and
[Nextafter], named as [math] names them, and the correctly rounded [Exp], [Log], [Log10], [Pow], [Pown],
[Rootn] and [Compound].

For fixed-scale arithmetic, such as money in cents, [QuoQuantize] divides
and rounds once to a given quantum, and [Decompose] and [Compose] convert
between a decimal value and its sign, coefficient and exponent. Its
[RoundingMode] has the names and values of [math/big.RoundingMode],
including [AwayFromZero], which rounds every inexact magnitude up.

[TotalOrder] and [TotalOrderMag] are the totalOrder and totalOrderMag
operations of IEEE 754-2019, which order every pair of values, NaNs
included, and [TotalCompare] and [TotalCompareMag] are TotalOrder and
TotalOrderMag as three-way comparisons for [slices.SortFunc].

Of the non-computational operations of IEEE 754-2019 clause 5.7.2, the
package has [Class], which returns one of the ten classes of
[IEEE754Class], the predicates [IsFinite], [IsZero], [IsNormal],
[IsSubnormal] and [IsCanonical], and [Radix], the formats' radix, 10.
[IsCanonicalBID128] and [IsCanonicalDPD128] report whether a decimal128
interchange encoding is canonical before it is converted.
